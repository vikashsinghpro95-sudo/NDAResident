GENERATION I // 1914 – 1918 ERA


--------------------------------------------------
[HEADING 2] The Great War Sapper & 85-Acre Gallantry Grant
--------------------------------------------------
The roots of the Pawar family’s devotion to the uniform began during the First World War. Serving as a combat engineer with the distinguished Bombay Engineering Group (BEG) — the renowned "Bombay Sappers" of the British Indian Army — the founder’s grandfather stood on frontline theatres.

For his gallantry in action, he was formally awarded an official British Indian military grant of 85 acres of land in Sangli district, Maharashtra. This historic land grant was not merely property; it was a permanent regimental symbol of courage under fire that instilled discipline as the core pillar of the Pawar family.

HISTORIC RECORD: BEG SAPPERS • 85-ACRE GALLANTRY CITATION • SANGLI DISTRICT

GENERATION II // MEMORIAL DEDICATION


--------------------------------------------------
[HEADING 2] Ex-Risaldar MajorM. K. Pawar
--------------------------------------------------
"A Soldier. A Father. A Blessing."

61ST CAVALRY (ARMOURED CORPS) • RECRUITED AT JAIPUR

Recruited at Jaipur into the legendary 61st Cavalry (Armoured Corps) — the world’s last operational horse-mounted cavalry regiment — Ex-Risaldar Major M. K. Pawar represented the pinnacle of regimental dignity.

As a master horse riding instructor and equestrian mentor, his bearing, punctuality, and unwavering adherence to truth shaped the upbringing of his son, Prof. Ravindra Pawar. This academy was conceived not as a business venture, but as a living military memorial to honour his life and pass his soldier’s spirit to future Indian Army officers.

Administrative Note: The Academy is named in solemn tribute to Ex-Risaldar Major M. K. Pawar. He is honoured as the inspirational patron and moral compass of the institution.

EXAMINATION MECHANICS


--------------------------------------------------
[HEADING 2] Stage I: The UPSC Written Blueprint
--------------------------------------------------
Total Marks: 900 • Duration: 5 Hours (Two Papers on the Same Day)

PAPER 01
300 MARKS // 2.5 HOURS

>>> [HEADING 3] Mathematics
120 Questions (2.5 marks each; negative 0.83 marks). Cut-off qualifying score is mandatory (~25% minimum per paper, target 130+ marks).


  • [SUBHEADING] Key High-Yield Modules:

  * Calculus: Differential, Integral, Differential Equations (28–32 Qs)
  * Trigonometry: Identities, Inverse, Heights & Distances (15–18 Qs)
  * Algebra: Matrices & Determinants, Complex Numbers, Sequences (25–30 Qs)
  * Vector Algebra & 3D Geometry (10–12 Qs)
  * Statistics & Probability (12–15 Qs)

PAPER 02
600 MARKS // 2.5 HOURS

>>> [HEADING 3] General Ability Test (GAT)
150 Questions (4 marks each; negative 1.33 marks). Divided into Part A (English) and Part B (General Knowledge & Science).


  • [SUBHEADING] Key Syllabus Breakdown:

  * Part A: English (200 Marks / 50 Qs) — Spotting Errors, Vocabulary, Synonyms/Antonyms, Comprehension
  * Part B: Physics (100 Marks / 25 Qs) — Mechanics, Optics, Electricity, Heat & Sound
  * Part B: Chemistry (60 Marks / 15 Qs) — Atomic structure, Acids/Bases, Physical chemistry
  * Part B: General Science / Biology (40 Marks / 10 Qs)
  * Part B: History, Polity, Geography & Current Affairs (200 Marks / 50 Qs)


--- [SECTION: #section-3 (section--dark)] ---
STAGE II // 5-DAY TESTING MATRIX


--------------------------------------------------
[HEADING 2] The Five Days of SSB Testing
--------------------------------------------------
Conducted at Services Selection Boards across India (Allahabad, Bhopal, Bangalore, Kapurthala, Gandhinagar, Dehradun).

DAY 01

>>> [HEADING 3] Screening Test (Stage I)
The first elimination hurdle. Only screened-in candidates stay for the remaining 4 days.


  * OIR: Officer Intelligence Rating Tests (Verbal & Non-verbal reasoning)
  * PPDT: Picture Perception & Description Test (30 sec view, 4 min story)
  * Individual Story Narration & Group Discussion

DAY 02

>>> [HEADING 3] Psychological Battery
Written psychological assessments evaluating subconscious instincts and OLQs under time pressure.


  * TAT: Thematic Apperception Test (12 slides including 1 blank)
  * WAT: Word Association Test (60 words, 15 sec per word)
  * SRT: Situation Reaction Test (60 situations, 30 min)
  * SD: Self-Description Test (Parents, Teachers, Friends & Self views)

DAY 03

>>> [HEADING 3] Group Testing (GTO Day 1)
Outdoor practical tasks assessing team cooperation, practical intelligence, and physical grit.


  * GD: Group Discussions on national security
  * GPE: Group Planning Exercise (map problem)
  * PGT: Progressive Group Task with planks & ropes
  * HGT: Snake Race & Obstacle Relay

DAY 04

>>> [HEADING 3] GTO Day 2 & Interviews
Individual command tests and personal interviews with the President of the Board.


  * Command Task: Command subordinates over obstacle
  * Individual Obstacles: 10 physical obstacles in 3 mins
  * FGT: Final Group Task
  * Personal Interview: In-depth PIQ interrogation

DAY 05

>>> [HEADING 3] Board Conference & Results
All assessors (Psychologist, GTO, Interviewing Officer) convene in full uniform to finalize recommendations.


  * Candidate appears before the entire board for brief confirmation questions
  * Final compilation of scores across all 3 testing techniques
  * Announcement of Recommended Candidates
  * Recommended candidates proceed to Military Medical Board

THE 15 OFFICER LIKE QUALITIES (OLQs)


--------------------------------------------------
[HEADING 2] The Four Factors of Leadership
--------------------------------------------------
Every assessment in SSB evaluates whether these 15 core leadership traits are naturally demonstrated.

FACTOR I

>>> [HEADING 3] Planning & Organising

  * Effective Intelligence: Practical solutions to complex hurdles.
  * Reasoning Ability: Grasping essentials and logical thinking.
  * Organising Ability: Arranging resources methodically.
  * Power of Expression: Lucid oral and written delivery.

FACTOR II

>>> [HEADING 3] Social Adjustment

  * Social Adaptability: Fitting into diverse soldier cultures.
  * Co-operation: Selfless team contribution over ego.
  * Sense of Responsibility: Extreme duty consciousness.

FACTOR III

>>> [HEADING 3] Social Effectiveness

  * Initiative: Taking the first step in unfamiliar tasks.
  * Self-Confidence: Inner faith in critical scenarios.
  * Speed of Decision: Swift resolution under battle chaos.
  * Ability to Influence the Group: Natural moral authority.
  * Liveliness: High cheerfulness despite physical fatigue.

FACTOR IV

>>> [HEADING 3] Dynamic & Stamina

  * Determination: Sustained effort without quitting.
  * Courage: Mental grit to take calculated physical risks.
  * Stamina: Capacity to endure prolonged physical strain.

FREQUENTLY ANSWERED QUESTIONS


--------------------------------------------------
[HEADING 2] Clarifications on Admission & Selection
--------------------------------------------------
[Action/Button: Can female candidates apply for the NDA residential batch?
+]
Yes. Following Supreme Court and Ministry of Defence orders, female cadets are fully eligible for the UPSC NDA examination and are welcomed into our dedicated residential training programs with segregated quarters and mentoring.

[Action/Button: What is the age limit for the UPSC NDA examination?
+]
Candidates must be between 16.5 and 19.5 years of age on the day the course commences at the National Defence Academy, Khadakwasla. Typically, students appear for NDA 1 and NDA 2 during Class 12 or immediately after passing 12th.

[Action/Button: Does the Academy guarantee selection in the SSB?
+]
Absolutely not. Any academy claiming guaranteed selection is unethical and false. Selection into the Armed Forces is decided solely by the Union Public Service Commission, Services Selection Boards, and Armed Forces Medical Boards. We provide world-class preparatory coaching, physical conditioning, and personality refinement.

[HEADING 2] The Five-Step Enrolment File
--------------------------------------------------
A clear step-by-step pathway from initial application to battalion fall-in.


  * 01

Dossier Submission & Initial Registration
Submit candidate details via the admission form below or visit the campus admissions cell. Provide 10th/12th academic marksheets and photo identification.
  * 02

Academic & Physical Baseline Diagnostic
Cadets undergo an aptitude diagnostic in basic mathematics, logical reasoning, and English, followed by a preliminary physical fitness evaluation (run, push-ups, chin-ups).
  * 03

Director Counselling & Parent Orientation
One-on-one session with Academic Director Prof. Ravindra Pawar to align expectations, choose the integrated curriculum pathway, and discuss residential boarding rules.
  * 04

Barracks Allotment & Kit Issue
Allotment of cadet dormitory bed, study table, and regimental training kit (PT rig, squad uniform, notebook satchels, and DPP material).
  * 05

Battalion Fall-In & 05:30 Reveille Induction
Formal induction into the Academy cohort. The cadet begins the transformative 24-hour regimental schedule under direct faculty and physical instructors.
