# Imagery & Archival Asset Mapping Guide
**Ex-Risaldar Major M. K. Pawar NDA & Defence Academy, Pune**

All imagery on this website adheres to strict **Honesty and Documentary Standards**:
1. Authentic 35mm documentary photojournalism aesthetic (warm low sun, cool ink-blue shadows, authentic grain, shallow depth of field).
2. **Honesty Rule**: No invented student portraits, no fake faculty, no false selection claims. All legacy visuals (BEG First World War era, 61st Cavalry memorial) are symbolic artifacts captioned *"Illustrative archival image"* or *"Illustrative memorial image"*.
3. **Easy Swap**: Real photos of the academy campus, cadet activities, and faculty can be swapped by placing the files in this directory with matching filenames or updating `config.js`.
