Terms of Service & Admission Ethics

SESSION 2026-27 • NARHE, PUNE

1. Non-Negotiable Selection Disclaimer

Ex-Risaldar Major M. K. Pawar NDA & Defence Academy is an independent educational training institution. Selection into the National Defence Academy (NDA), Indian Military Academy (IMA), Air Force Academy (AFA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), or Officer Training Academy (OTA) is decided solely by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Services Selection Boards (SSBs), and competent Armed Forces medical boards. The Academy provides preparatory guidance, academic coaching, and physical conditioning; it does not and cannot guarantee selection.

2. Cadet Code of Conduct & 05:30 Reveille

Cadets admitted to residential programs are subject to regimental disciplinary standards. Punctual turnout for the 05:30 Reveille muster, mandatory attendance in supervised self-study hours, respectful conduct towards faculty and peers, and strict compliance with the mobile phone policy are conditions of continued enrollment.

3. Medical & Physical Fitness Acknowledgment

Parents and candidates acknowledge that physical training, cross-country stamina runs, and military obstacle courses involve inherent physical exercise. Candidates must disclose any pre-existing medical conditions, surgical histories, or cardiovascular limitations prior to battalion induction.

4. Jurisdiction

Any administrative disputes or contractual inquiries related to enrollment shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent civil courts in Pune, Maharashtra, India.
